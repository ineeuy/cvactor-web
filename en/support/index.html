How to…

How do I get my subscription back on another phone?

Open the Settings tab, tap “See plans” and then “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, tap “Delete my data”. All your resumes, photos and settings are deleted from your phone. This doesn’t cancel your subscription: you do that from your App Store subscription settings.

FAQ

Do I need design skills?

No. You fill in a simple form and the design builds itself, in the style you choose.

Does it work for dubbing and commercials?

Yes. Choose your profile (acting, dubbing & voice, or commercials) and the resume asks for the details used in each case.

Which countries does it work in?

Argentina, Uruguay, Mexico, Brazil, the United States and the United Kingdom. The resume uses the labels and details each market asks for.

Can I change styles later?

Anytime. Your info stays saved and the resume is redesigned instantly.

Who sees my resume?

Only the people who get your PDF. It doesn’t show up on Google or any search engine, and we can’t see it either.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There’s no account and no server: to move your resumes to another phone, export a backup from Settings and import it on the new one.

How do I cancel?

From your phone’s subscription settings (App Store), anytime. You keep access until the end of the paid period, and your resumes stay on your phone.
