Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what personal data CV Actor uses, where it is kept and what rights you have over it. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own: what you enter stays on your device. The data controller is Inés Aguiar.

What data the app uses

Resume data: what you enter, such as contact details, photos, physical details, experience, training and skills.

Preferences: country and language, the plan you chose and whether you have already seen the introduction.

Region: taken from your device settings. We do not look up your IP address or your location.

We do not ask you to create an account, or for a username, password or email address, to use the app.

Where it is stored

Only on your device, in the app’s private storage. We have no servers that receive or store your resume, your photos or your preferences, and we have no access to them.

The PDF is generated on your device. Fonts and styles are bundled with the app, so creating your resume or the PDF does not send your data to anyone.

Phone backups

If you have iCloud Backup turned on, the system may include the app’s data in that backup. The backup is stored in your own Apple account, under Apple’s policies, and we have no access to it.

You can turn off these backups in your device settings.

When you share

When you share a PDF or export a backup, the file leaves the app for the destination you choose: WhatsApp, email, a folder or a cloud service. From then on, how it is handled depends on that service and on whoever receives it.

The backup contains all your resumes and photos. Keep it somewhere you trust.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not share it with production companies or agencies. You decide who you send your resume to.

Payments

Subscriptions are purchased and paid for only through the App Store (Apple). Apple handles your payment data under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card details.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app shows no advertising and includes no cookies, analytics or tracking tools, or any other third-party services that receive your data.

Your rights

Because your data is only on your device, you can view, correct, export and delete it directly in the app. In Settings, the Delete my data option erases everything; uninstalling the app also removes it from the device.

For any question, or to exercise your rights, write to us at hola@cvactor.com. Because we do not store your data, we will generally hold no information about you other than what you send us by email.

The law of your country and the authority you can complain to are described below.

Retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. PDFs and backups you have shared or exported, and iCloud backups, remain wherever you saved them.

If you write to us, we keep your email for as long as needed to reply and to comply with the law.

Minors

Resumes of minors must be created and managed by their parent or legal guardian.

Depending on your country

Argentina · Law 25,326

The data subject has the right to access their personal data free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown, as provided in Article 14, paragraph 3 of Law No. 25,326.

The Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the Supervisory Authority under Law No. 25,326, has the power to handle complaints and claims filed by anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the rules on personal data protection.

Uruguay · Law 18,331

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, updating, inclusion and erasure of your data from the app or by writing to us.

If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can file a complaint with the Personal Data Regulatory and Control Unit (Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales, URCDP).

Mexico · Purposes

Primary purposes, necessary for the service: creating and saving your resume on your device and generating the PDF. This processing takes place on your device; we do not receive your data.

We do not process your data for secondary purposes such as advertising or marketing.

Mexico · ARCO rights

To exercise your rights of Access, Rectification, Cancellation or Opposition, or to withdraw your consent, send an email to hola@cvactor.com with your name, the right you wish to exercise and a way for us to reply. We will answer within the time limits set by the Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares).

If you believe your request was not properly handled, you can turn to the Secretariat of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance (Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno), the competent authority in this area.

Brazil · LGPD (Law 13,709/2018)

Legal basis: we process your data to perform the contract for the service you requested.

You can confirm whether processing takes place, and access, correct, anonymize, port or delete your data and withdraw your consent, in the app or by writing to us.

Data protection officer (encarregado): Inés Aguiar · hola@cvactor.com

We do not send your data to servers of our own. The App Store and iCloud backups may process data outside Brazil, under Apple’s policies and the safeguards provided in the LGPD.

You can also file a complaint with the National Data Protection Authority (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados, ANPD).

United States · Your privacy

We do not sell or share your personal information, including for targeted advertising, because we do not receive it: what you enter stays on your device.

You can access, correct, export and delete your data directly in the app. For any other request about your data, write to hola@cvactor.com and we will respond as required under applicable law.

The app is not intended for children to use on their own. A parent or legal guardian must create and manage a minor’s resume.

United Kingdom · Data protection

Data controller: Inés Aguiar · Uruguay · hola@cvactor.com

We use your data only to provide the service you request, and that processing takes place on your device: we do not receive your data, and we do not sell or share it.

Under applicable UK data protection law, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can do most of this directly in the app, or by writing to us.

If you use iCloud backups or share files through other services, those companies may store them outside the United Kingdom, under their own safeguards.

If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection authority.
